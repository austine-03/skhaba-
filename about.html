About Skhaba Gallery

We are passionate storytellers dedicated to capturing life's most precious moments through the art of photography and videography

Our Story

Founded in 2019, Skhaba Gallery began as a passion project between friends who shared a love for capturing authentic moments. What started as weekend photography sessions has grown into a full-service creative studio specializing in weddings, portraits, events, and video production.

Our name "Skhaba" means "to shine" in Zulu, reflecting our mission to help our clients shine in every frame. We believe that every moment has a story worth telling, and we're here to tell it beautifully.

Today, we've expanded our services to include music video production, podcast recording, and commercial videography, always maintaining our core values of authenticity, creativity, and excellence.
