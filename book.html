  • • A 50% non-refundable deposit is required to secure your booking date

  • • Late payment may result in cancellation of services
  • • All prices are subject to applicable taxes
  • • Payment can be made via bank transfer, credit card, or cash

2. Cancellation Policy

  • • Cancellations more than 30 days before event: 50% refund of total payment
  • • Cancellations 15-30 days before event: 25% refund of total payment
  • • Cancellations less than 15 days before event: No refund
  • • Deposit is non-refundable in all circumstances
  • • Rescheduling is allowed once without penalty (subject to availability)

3. Service Delivery

  • • Standard delivery time is 2-3 weeks for edited photos/videos
