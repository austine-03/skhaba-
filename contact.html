Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking 3-6 months in advance for weddings and 2-4 weeks for other sessions to ensure availability.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes! We travel locally within 50 miles at no extra charge. For destinations beyond that, we can discuss travel arrangements.

How long until I receive my photos?

Turnaround time is typically 2-3 weeks for weddings and 1-2 weeks for other sessions. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.

What's included in your packages?

All packages include professional editing, high-resolution digital gallery, and print release. Specific inclusions vary by package type.
